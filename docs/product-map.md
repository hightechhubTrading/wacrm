# HTH product map

Version 3, updated 17 November 2026. This version is built the way a doors, shutters and gates business organises its range, and is not derived from the company archive. The archive was used only to check that nothing the company sells is missing. Prices are left out on purpose: sales set them on each quotation. The initial inventory migration was applied on 17 November 2026; application work and verification continue in [the execution record](product-map-execution.md).

## 1. How the range is organised

1. **Group by what the customer wants to close or cover.** A window, a garage, a shopfront, a driveway, a front entrance, a room, a terrace. Customers ask this way ("I need a garage door"), so staff find the product in one step.
2. **Material is a choice, not a category.** Steel or aluminium, plain or decorative, are answers inside a product. The two exceptions are aluminium and uPVC windows, because customers in Qatar ask for them by material.
3. **Four shelves, never mixed.** Products, Add-ons, Services, Workshop parts.
4. **Products have three levels only.** Category → Product → Choices.
5. **One product, one record.** A roller shutter for a shop and the same shutter for a warehouse are one product with a "used for" label.
6. **Every product asks the same four questions first.** Size (width × height), Operation (manual or motorized), Colour or finish, Used for. Product-specific questions come after. Every product is supplied and installed by HTH; there is no supply-only option.
7. **Sales choose the motor type; the size of the opening decides the motor model.** Nobody should guess a motor by kilograms on a quotation.
8. **Chat photos come from Products only.** Never from add-ons, services or parts.
9. **Each product has a simple English name, the Arabic word staff already use, and a code.**

## 3. Products in detail

The four standard questions (size, operation, colour, used for) apply to every product and are not repeated below.

### 1. Window Shutters · شتر النوافذ

The product is the slat (الشريحة). All three are double-wall aluminium slats filled with insulating foam; the number is the slat height in millimetres. HTH writes them as "Model RC 41", "RC 55" and "RC 77".

| Product | Staff also say | Notes from the company's own quotations |
|---|---|---|
| Window Shutter 41 | RC 41، RC 40، شريحة 41 | Smallest slat. Sometimes written 40 or 44; treated as the same slat. |
| Window Shutter 55 | RC 55، شريحة 55 | The standard slat: named in most quotations and in every workshop order sheet. |
| Window Shutter 77 | RC 77، شريحة 77 | Largest slat, for wide openings. Has its own line in the April 2026 price list. |

Source: 220 readable window-shutter quotations (2023 to 2026), the April 2026 price list (one rate for 41 and 55, another for 77) and the workshop order sheets. Supplier price comparisons also list 39 mm and 45 mm slats, but no customer quotation names them, so they are not products.

Company answer supplied on 17 November 2026 for Window Shutter 41 and Window Shutter 55:

| Specification | Window Shutter 41 | Window Shutter 55 |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Aluminium | Aluminium |
| Slat size | 41 mm | 55 mm |
| Stated slat thickness | 4 mm | 5 mm |
| Standard colours | Beige · White | Beige · White |
| Heat insulation | High degree of heat insulation | High degree of heat insulation |
| Light blocking | 100% | 100% |
| Dust insulation | Dust insulation | Dust insulation |
| Sound insulation | Good degree of sound insulation | Good degree of sound insulation |

These are company-supplied specifications and performance claims. No numerical thermal or acoustic rating was supplied. “Thickness” retains the company's wording for the slat; it is not recorded as aluminium wall/gauge thickness. The source's “عازل صور” is normalized to “عازل صوت” (sound insulation) as a spelling correction. This answer applies to 41 and 55 only; it does not establish the thickness, colours or performance of 77.

## 10. Decisions for the company

1. **Switch off what is not sold.** Sliding Door, Folding Door, the uPVC split, and the after-sale services are standard for this trade but are not confirmed by company records. Any product the company does not offer should be hidden, not deleted.
2. **Standard package per product.** Section 7 needs the company to state what is included in each product by default. This is the single biggest cause of inconsistent quotations.
3. **Motor rule.** Someone technical should write the simple rule "opening size → motor model" for categories 1, 2, 3 and 5, so sales never pick a motor by guesswork.
