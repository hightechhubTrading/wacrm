# Shared knowledge and quotation inventory

Knowledge (`Settings → Products`) owns each product identity, name, SKU, specifications, review status and price range. `catalog_items` is a database-maintained quotation projection of the same UUID; authenticated clients cannot edit it independently. Future quotation selections read this projection. Existing quotation items, source snapshots and issued PDFs retain their saved details.

A fixed quotation default exists only for an approved, customer-visible item whose recorded minimum equals its maximum. Ranges are shown as estimates and require a quotation-specific amount. Family approval and customer visibility apply to descendants. Internal components and equipment remain review drafts until explicitly approved; importing a supplier catalogue does not assert that HTH supplies it. Saving a custom quotation item creates a shared internal draft rather than publishing facts to the assistant.

`product_source_links` attaches immutable archive evidence to the exact inventory record. Versioned source approval remains separate from product approval. Product presets resolve the current shared product by ID before filling a new quotation line. Archive family links provide context and do not automatically transfer a price, attachment, warranty or specification to a variant.

`catalog_question_findings` records source evidence, coverage and what remains unanswered alongside staff questions. Importing findings never changes a staff answer, confirmation, or open/closed status. Findings and unresolved points are included in the collection workbook.

Migration: `091_shared_product_inventory.sql`. Deploy preflight requires its fields and relationship tables before promotion. Original legacy catalogue values are retained in the corresponding draft product's `source_notes`. A product referenced by quotations must be retained; withdraw its approval rather than deleting it.

Private reconciliation plans live outside Git under `.local/`. `scripts/apply-shared-inventory-plan.mjs` defaults to a dry run; applying checks the linked project, account owner/admin, exact source version/count and unchanged reviewed question timestamps, then inserts one transaction. It never overwrites existing canonical facts or source approvals. Snapshot before applying. Reapplication is idempotent by deterministic product IDs, gap keys and exact source relationships.

Validation: `scripts/check-shared-inventory-db.mjs` runs disposable PostgreSQL integration checks (set `PGLITE_MODULE` when the dependency is installed outside the project). `scripts/check-shared-inventory-browser.mjs` renders the real inventory and collection screens with synthetic data in English/Arabic, light/dark, at 390/1440 pixels. It checks item editing, linked evidence, partial findings and preservation of blank staff answers/open status. Use `PLAYWRIGHT_CHANNEL=chrome` when local Playwright browser binaries are unavailable.
