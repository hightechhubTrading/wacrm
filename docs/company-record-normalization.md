# Company records and normalization

The supplied company archive is authoritative evidence of the products, prices, transactions, codes and practices it records. SYS cleans and organizes that evidence. It does not ask the company to approve its own records again merely because the wording or extraction is messy.

Normalize spelling, grammar, bilingual extraction fragments, units of presentation and duplicate descriptions while retaining item identity, manufacturer codes, dimensions, amounts and original references. Prefer an explicit structured field over an inconsistent heading: for example, a Copaco sunscreen fabric identifies the GTR sunscreen configuration even where a copied heading says strong flyscreen. Do not infer a missing amount, currency, purchasing unit, model rating or price-column policy from cleaner wording.

The shared inventory's internal `approved` state means the record is documented and prepared. `customer_visible` independently controls use in customer knowledge and quotation selection. Components and supplier ranges can be documented internal inventory without being incomplete records. A generic family is not a specific quotation configuration. Original identifiers and supplier aliases remain attached; superseded duplicates remain recoverable.

Source `review_status` records preparation/acceptance of evidence. It is not a claim that the company signed off on every field or that an automatic calculation is ready. `sourceReadyToApply` additionally requires a product mapping, selling-price rule or complete supplier-cost configuration, as appropriate. Server-side price and landed-cost functions independently enforce those calculation requirements. Unspecified O/P/R/S columns, EUR/QAR conversion, purchasing units and landed charges remain open settings; no default is guessed. Raw source data and quotation snapshot labels stay unchanged. `recordDisplayLabel` and the detail fields provide the readable view, with the original record available for traceability.

Open questions contain actual missing facts, conflicting evidence and decisions. Answers already established by the archive belong in recorded answers and the matching catalogue item. Partial answers can coexist with a remaining open detail. Staff-confirmed answers and previously closed questions retain their attribution and state.

`apply-company-normalization.mjs` consumes a reviewed private plan, defaults to a dry run, checks the account/project, source processing version and product/question freshness, and applies one transaction. Only names, descriptions, preparation state, visibility, hierarchy, aliases and provenance can change on existing products. Prices, SKUs, quoted items, issued PDFs, original source rows and reference sequences are outside its mutation set. Company product-code labels are standardized while their codes are retained. Source acceptance uses the existing versioned audit RPC.
